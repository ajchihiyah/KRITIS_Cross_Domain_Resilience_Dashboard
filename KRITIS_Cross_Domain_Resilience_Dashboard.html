⚖️ Regulatory Foundation

KRITIS-DachG (in force March 17, 2026) + NIS2UmsuCG — the dual compliance regime

KRITIS-DachG
In Force

Gesetz zur Stärkung der Resilienz Kritischer Anlagen — implements EU Directive 2022/2557 (CER Directive). The first German law mandating physical and organizational resilience across sectors.

Enforcement: BBK (Bundesamt für Bevölkerungsschutz und Katastrophenhilfe)

Scope: ~2,000 operators across 11+ sectors

Threshold: Generally 500,000 inhabitants supply volume

NIS2UmsuCG
Complementary

Gesetz zur Umsetzung der NIS2-Richtlinie — governs digital cybersecurity. KRITIS-DachG operators are generally deemed "besonders wichtige Einrichtungen" under BSIG §28 Abs. 1 Nr. 1.

Enforcement: BSI (Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik)

Scope: Digital cybersecurity, network security, incident response

Relationship: Both laws can apply simultaneously to the same operator

📜 Key Legal Sections

Registration & 24/7 Contact Point
SECTION_8
  • Registration via joint BBK/BSI platform (BSI-Gesetz portal)
  • Operator master data, facility class, threshold evidence
  • 24/7 contact point for authorities (§8 Abs. 1 Nr. 6)
  • Updates within 2 weeks for changes; annual for supply degree changes
  • BBK can register operators ex officio if they fail to comply
Risk Analysis (4-Year Cycle)
SECTION_12
  • Comprehensive risk assessment minimum every 4 years
  • Must cover: natural disasters, technical failures, anthropogenic risks, hybrid threats, terrorism
  • Must consider federal national risk analysis
  • First due 9 months after registration
  • BMI may issue methodological regulations and templates
Resilience Plan & Measures
SECTION_13
  • Appropriate technical, organizational, and security measures (TOM)
  • Documented purpose-means proportionality assessment
  • Resilience plan kept current
  • BBK will provide templates and models
  • First due 10 months after registration
Evidence & Audit
SECTION_16
  • No regular mandatory audits by operators themselves
  • BBK/zuständige Behörde can request evidence (resilience plan, audit results)
  • Can request BSI insight into §39 KRITIS-Nachweis (NIS2)
  • Risk-based audits by authorities (no fixed intervals)
  • Can demand deficiency remediation plans
  • Can engage independent third parties
  • BBK will define audit methodology and auditor requirements
Incident Reporting (Dual Reporting)
SECTION_18
  • Incidents with significant effects must be reported without undue delay, within 24 hours maximum
  • Detailed report within 1 month (72 hours in some interpretations for initial detailed follow-up)
  • Report to joint BBK/BSI reporting point
  • Content: nature, cause, consequences, affected users, duration, geographic area, cross-border effects
  • BBK and BSI coordinate reporting procedure details
  • BBK forwards to EU authorities if 6+ member states affected
Management/Board Duties
SECTION_20
  • Management must organize and supervise implementation of resilience measures
  • Personal liability under corporate law for breaches
  • Analogous to NIS2 Article 34 board liability
  • Liable with private assets for known or should-have-known breaches
  • Management ban up to 5 years possible in repeat cases

M1: Unified Incident Correlation Engine
M1

Real-time correlation of cyber alerts (SIEM, IDS, EDR, firewall, AD) with physical security events (access control, CCTV motion detection, perimeter breach sensors, BMS environmental alerts). Uses temporal proximity, spatial relationship, asset mapping, and user identity linking.

Correlation Scenarios

Insider Threat BOTH (BSI + BBK)

Unauthorized physical access + failed/successful remote login from unusual location + data exfiltration attempts

Example: Server Room A badge breach at 02:00 + Admin VPN failure from external IP at 02:05 + unusual outbound data spike

OT/ICS Physical Compromise BOTH (BSI + BBK)

Physical intrusion into control room + SCADA network anomaly + process parameter changes

Example: Control room motion detection + PLC communication anomaly + valve position unauthorized change

Supply Chain Attack with Physical Access BOTH (BSI + BBK)

Vendor/service provider physical access + malware deployment on internal systems

Example: Maintenance contractor badge entry + new executable on engineering workstation

Drone/Surveillance Incident BOTH (BSI + BBK)

Unauthorized drone overflight + network scanning/RF interference detected

Example: Perimeter radar detects drone + WiFi/RF anomaly detected by IDS

Natural Disaster with Cyber Impact BBK (primary) + BSI

Environmental sensor alert (flood, fire, earthquake) + IT system failures

Example: BMS flood detection basement + server room temperature spike + network segment down

M2: Dual-Reporting Decision Tree & Workflow
M2

Interactive decision engine that guides analysts through §18 KRITIS-DachG reporting classification. Automatically suggests BSI-only, BBK-only, or dual reporting based on incident correlation results.

Reporting Decision Logic

BSI Only
  • • Pure IT security incident (malware, data breach, unauthorized network access)
  • • No physical security component
  • • No impact on physical processes or facility safety
  • • Digital-only supply chain compromise
BBK Only
  • • Pure physical incident (natural disaster, sabotage, terrorism, physical damage)
  • • No IT systems compromised or affected
  • • Personnel safety incident without digital component
  • • Physical perimeter breach without network access
Both Authorities
  • • OT/ICS compromise affecting physical processes
  • • Insider threat with digital + physical components
  • • Unauthorized drone overflight with RF/cyber interference
  • • Supply chain attack with physical access + digital payload
  • • Environmental disaster causing both physical damage and IT outage
  • • Physical theft of IT assets containing sensitive data

Workflow Steps

1. Incident auto-classification based on correlation engine output
Step 2
2. Analyst review with manual override capability
Step 3
3. Authority selection (BSI / BBK / Both) with legal justification
Step 4

7. Digital signature and submission to joint BBK/BSI portal

Interactive facility visualization showing real-time cyber-physical risk posture. Overlays cyber asset locations, physical sensor status, personnel positions, and environmental conditions on facility floor plans.

  • Click-through to asset/sensor detail with event history
  • Evacuation route overlay for emergency response
